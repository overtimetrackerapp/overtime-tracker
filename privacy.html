Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Overtime Tracker is built so your information stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app handles and what it does not.

What stays on your device

Your shifts, pay periods, pay rates, settings, and widget data are stored on your iPhone, inside the app and a shared storage area used by the app's widget and Siri shortcut. We do not run a server for the app, we do not have accounts, and we cannot see or retrieve your entries.

Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive your name, email, location, calendar, pay data, or usage data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking software.

Calendar access

If you turn on calendar sync, the app asks for permission to use your calendars. It reads events whose titles match your keyword and turns them into shifts on your device. If you use the Siri option that adds a shift to your calendar, the app also creates calendar events. Calendar content is never sent to us. You can change or revoke access in Settings, Privacy & Security, Calendars.

Notifications

Pro timecard reminders and rest guard alerts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you log overtime by voice, Siri and Apple process your speech under Apple's own privacy terms. The app receives only the resulting text, such as the length of the shift. See Apple's privacy information for Siri.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details and your purchase record. The app checks locally whether you have an active subscription. The developer does not receive your payment information.

Exports and backups

Pro lets you export your entries as CSV or JSON and back them up to a file. These files are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to save or share them. Once a file leaves the app, its handling is up to the place you send it.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we do not have it.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete entries or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Backups you made through Apple's own iPhone or iCloud backup are controlled by your Apple Account settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Check this page for the current version.
